## [v2026] - 2026-08-19

2026-utgaven, undervist på MGVM4100 høsten 2026.
[Release](https://github.com/roarstovner/kvant_for_laerere/releases/tag/v2026),
DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22002981](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22002981).
